Panel and beam foot 
attaches to end-of-run and 
in-line junctions to support 
a freestanding panel run.

post is offset on the panel 
and beam foot and allows 
for a choice of proud or inset 
installation at the end-of-run 
by rotating the panel and 
beam foot.

positioned proud of the end 
of run accommodates Ology, 
Migration, and Airtouch

is used parallel to the panel 
without overlapping table

cleaner visual and accom-
