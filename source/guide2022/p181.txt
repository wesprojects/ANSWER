Three wiring schemat-
ics are available–3+1, 2+2, 
and three circuits with sep-
arate neutrals (3SN). All the 
components in an electrical 
distribution system must use 
the same wiring schematic.  
For safety the components 
are keyed, labeled, and col-
or-coded to make it impossi-
ble to connect mismatched 
parts.

by Underwriters Laboratory

components.
