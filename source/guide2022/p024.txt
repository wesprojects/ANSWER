height trim is used at 
end-of-run or in-line change-
of-height. The top of the trim 
aligns with the top cap on 
the panel.
An end-of-run aligner is 
included with the end-of-run 
change-of-height trim to pro-
vide tight alignment between 
the top cap and the change-
of-height trim. No junction 
cap is required.
L corner change-of-
height trim is used in a T 
or X change-of-height appli-
cation when there are two 
panels at the tallest height 
located adjacent to each 
other. No aligners are requi-
red to connect the L corner 
change-of-height trim to the 
junction cap.

change-of-height trim resides

The L to T aligner creates a 
tight connection between the 
two trim pieces.
