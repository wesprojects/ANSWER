Steelcase carefully selects veneer and solid wood 
for consistent color and grain structure. Wood is a 
natural material and variations will occur in color, 
grain and texture. These variations are part of the 
inherent natural beauty of wood and are not con-
sidered defects.
All wood products will darken with age and expo-
sure to ultraviolet light. This is especially apparent 
with cherry and maple veneer. We recommend 
that desk accessories be rearranged periodically 
to ensure even aging of wood surfaces.
When storing your wood furniture, please follow 
the following guidelines:
• Do not store products in trailers
•  Store products in areas hat simulate ofÀ ce tem-
peratures (60°F to 90°F)
•  Store products in areas that maintain constant, 
ofÀ ce-like humidity levels
•  Keep product away from light. Cover products to 
make sure they are not exposed to light.
