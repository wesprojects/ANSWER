Boundary screens attach on the square edge of  universal straight,
transitional, tapered, bullet peninsula, corner, jetty, and bubble jetty.
Boundary screens are not intended for use on the depth edge of surfaces.

Maximum allowed width of a screen is determined by the installed 
height, screen clearance, and worksurface width.
To ensure stability, any boundary screen applied in a freestanding application 
must not exceed the size listed in matrix, on page 256.
Boundary screens are not permitted on height adjustable desks as 
they do not pass minimum safety requirements which could result in the 
desk tipping over.
