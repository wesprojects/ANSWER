Answer Boundary Screens
Boundary screen heights align with thin trim top cap panel heights at 30", 42", 48", and 54". 36"H 
boundary screens are also available.
Boundary screens have modular heights and widths. Parametric widths are allowed from 24"W to 
75"W increasing in 1¼16" increments. Boundary screen return widths are available at 18", 24", and 30", 
and are also available parametrically from 18"W to 75"W increasing in 1¼16" increments.
