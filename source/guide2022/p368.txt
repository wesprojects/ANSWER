Tip: Omit trim option omits 
all vertical and change-
of-height trim. This option 
is used when stacking on 
base junctions. Vertical 
and change-of-height trim 
should be speciÀ ed sepa-
rately to span the full height 
of the base junction plus 
stacking junction.
Tip: Remember to specify 
necessary junction caps 
and aligners if the omit trim 
option is selected.
Tip: Change-of-height junc-
tion ships right-handed. For 
a left-handed junction, a 
post will need to be moved 
during installation.
