Consolidation Point
Product Details
Utility pole can route 
cables from the ceiling of 
the building to the consoli-
dation point. Pole can also 
accommodate a power 
harness.
Mounting brackets 
accommodate standard- 
size, 19" patch panels or 
punch-down blocks which 
are supplied by the data/ 
voice cabling installer.
Space inside panel is 
3"D x 30"W or 36"W x 20"H.
Powerkits and cable 
trays cannot be used in the 
same location or height as a 
consolidation point kit.
Cable manager helps 
keep cables in order.
Connections
Hardware to attach 
consolidation point kit 
is included.
Wiring & Cabling
Consolidation point can 
typically accommodate up 
to 72 connections. Capacity 
depends on neatness of 
installation.
