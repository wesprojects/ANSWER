Individual wood skins 
are available for use in 
monolithic applications or in 
combination with other types 
of skins on the same frame.
Wood skin sets contain 
speciÀ c combinations of sizes 
and are used when segment-
ing all wood skins on 42"H,

skins on a frame.

to finish the appearance of 
a panel and can be used in 
combination with any other
