Change-of-height top
cap option must be speci-
fied on the lower panel. 
Square trim top caps
have a notch in one or both
ends to accommodate
change-of-height applications.
Oval trim top caps
are shortened on one or
both ends to accommodate
change-of-height 
applications.
Specify change-of-height
top caps at one end unless
the panel is adjacent to taller
panels on both sides. Then
specify a top cap change-of-
height on both ends.
Specify the tallest height
junction to connect your
panels. A 66"H junction
would be shared between
these panels.
