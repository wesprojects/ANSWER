Product Details
On-module worksur-
face supports engage 
the slots in the vertical
uprights of Kick panels.
Side support brackets
support worksurfaces at 
any height in 1" increments.
Brackets ship as a left- 
and right-hand pair and 
are ordered separately.
Side support brackets
can be used to support the
end of a worksurface that is
wrapped by a panel with the
same width dimension that
matches the worksurface
depth. 
Single side support
bracket can be used to
support the rear corner of
corner, extended corner, and
120° corner worksurfaces. It
is standard with these corner
worksurfaces.
