Fixed pedestals are
intended to attach under a
worksurface for security and
support. Three pedestal
depths are available to cor-
respond with Universal
Worksurface depths. Proud-
front pedestals exactly
match the depth of Universal
Worksurfaces. Flush-front
pedestals are 7⁄8" shorter.
Filler conceals the gap that
may exist between the face
of a panel and the back of a
27"H pedestal.  Fillers are
also used to provide stability
for pedestal and worksurface
conﬁgurations that are not
panel-wrapped. 
Tip: Fillers for proud front
pedestals conceal a 1⁄2" 
gap. Fillers for ﬂush front
pedestals conceal a 13⁄8"
gap. 
Pedestals may require
counterweights when used
in stand-alone desks.
