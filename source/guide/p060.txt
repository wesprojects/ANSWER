Product Details
Frameless glass
screen with clip
attachment is secured to
horizontal connecting bar by
supports below the top cap
and brackets above the top
cap. Two brackets are used
above the top cap to attach
the glass to the panel on 
24"–60"W panels. Three
brackets are used on panels
78" and wider.
 
Frameless glass
screens with clip
attachment can be used
on panels with thin 
profile trim.

cap with holes to accommo-
date brackets.
