Product Details
Closed, open, and half-
loop legs are handed
when panel supporting and
must be specified left or right
when facing the front of the
worksurface. Freestanding
tethered and table versions
are non-handed.
Closed, open, and half-
loop legs are available in
panel supporting versions.
They support the ends of
worksurface; they are not
shared supports between
two worksurfaces.
Closed and open-loop
legs are available in 
tethered versions in which
one side of the worksurface
is attached to a panel or 
storage.
