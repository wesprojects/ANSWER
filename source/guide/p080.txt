with stacking change-of-

Stacking change-of-
height trim has a straight
bottom edge to fit on top of
standard change-of-height trim.
Top cap on lower panel
must be change-of-height.
Remember to specify the
change-of-height top cap option
on the lower panel for either
square or oval trim applications.
Oval standard 
change-of-height trim
has a rounded bottom edge
to fit the curve of top caps.
Junction cap is included
with standard change-of-
height trim. Cap is available in
two styles - square and oval.
Top cap from base
panel is used to trim the
top of the stacked panel.
Product Details
Square standard slim
trim includes junction cap.
In-line or corner application
must be specified. Available
in 12"H, 18"H, 24"H, 30"H,
and 36"H. 
Oval standard slim trim
includes junction cap and
top cap filler. Plastic filler is
not the same filler that ships
with cable-routing trim. In-
line or corner application
must be specified. Available
in 12"H, 18"H, 24"H, 30"H,
and 36"H. 
Stacking slim trim
includes plastic aligner and
works with both square and
oval trim styles. Available in
12"H, 18"H, and 24"H.
