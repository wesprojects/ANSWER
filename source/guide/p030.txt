Product Details
Thin end-of-run vertical
trim should be specified for
the full height of the panel
(base height plus stacking
height, if stacking).
Thin end-of-run appli-
cations do not require a
junction cap as the end-of-
run trim connects with the
panel top cap.
Vertical trim aligners
to connect the end-of-run
vertical trim to the top cap
are included with the end-of-
run vertical trim.
Thin end-of-run vertical
trim can only be applied to
end-of-run junctions manu-
factured on or after October
10, 2011.
