Tip: 6"H box drawers come
standard with a divider 
package.
Tip: Some cabinets with the
c:scape glide base or
FrameOne foot base require
a different counterweight
than the Universal 3" base.
Please specify accordingly.
Tip: Counterweight pack ages
are recommended based on
case size and interior
options.
Tip: Counterweight option
should be specified if the
cabinet is not ganged back-
to-back to adjacent cabinets
or bolted to the floor or wall.
Cabinets ganged side-to-
side require a counterweight.
Tip: Counterweight pack-
ages ship separately from
case and must be installed
on site.

and will add 3⁄16" to overall

available on 28"H lateral
files only. FrameOne top will
add 1/2" to the overall height.
