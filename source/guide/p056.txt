Product Details
Frameless glass
screen with recessed
attachment is secured to
horizontal connecting bar by
supports below the top cap.
Two brackets are used
below the top cap to attach
the glass to the panel on 
24"–72"W panels. Three
brackets are used on panels
78" and wider.
 
Frameless glass
screens with recessed
attachment can be used
on panels with thin 
profile trim.
Frameless glass kit
includes glass screen, 
supports, and top caps.
Gap between adjacent
glass screens is 1/8".
