Tip: Junction includes:
• V change-of-height 
  junction
• V vertical trim
• 120° change-of-height trim
• 120° junction cap
• Aligner
Tip: Omit trim option omits 
all vertical and change-of-
height trim. This option is 
used when stacking on base
junctions. Vertical and
change-of-height trim should
be specified separately to
span the full height of the
base junction plus stacking
junction.
Tip: Remember to specify
necessary junction caps and
aligners if the omit trim
option is selected.
Tip: Change-of-height junc-
tion ships right-handed. For
a left-handed junction, a
post will need to be moved
during installation.
