Tip: Standard worksurfaces
are 13⁄16" thick. However,
the actual thickness
between Low-Pressure
Laminate (LPL) and High-
Pressure Laminate (HPL)
worksurfaces is slightly dif-
ferent. It is recommended
that only worksurfaces of the
same type are speciﬁed in a
given application. In addi-
tion, there may be sheen or
texture differences when
using LPL and HPL in adja-
cent applications. Please
use caution when mixing
and matching different work-
surface types.
