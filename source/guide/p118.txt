Glass windows provide a
visual space definition, yet
allow light to pass through
the workstation. They are
available in single pane or
double pane. Also available
is a single pane kit that ships
without glass and is used
with custom glass, ordered
separately.
cSpecifying, page 464
Safety glass is provided,
unless custom glass window
kit is ordered.
Metal frame is painted
and fully assembled.
