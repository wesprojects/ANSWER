Upmounted bin and
shelf height is 66"H when
installed on 54"H panels.
Upmount bin or shelf
width must match the width
of the panel or panels that it
attaches to. Upmounted bins
and shelves cannot be sup-
ported off-module.
Upmounted bin or shelf
can be attached in front of,
above or adjacent to a slat-
wall as long as there is not
another bin, shelf or another
slatwall skin on the adjacent
in-line panel.
Tip: When using upmount
brackets with overhead stor-
age and daisy chaining
lights, use the open design
upmount bracket for better
cord management.
Basic and open design
upmount brackets,
optional, attach bins and
shelves near the top of 54"H
panels.
Tip: Stationary shelf cannot
hang from an upmounted bin
or shelf.
