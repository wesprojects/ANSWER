The standard application direction of
fabric on Answer panels, skins, and Hutch kit
tackboard is warp horizontal. Standard Steelcase
fabrics were designed for this application 
direction.
The optional application direction of
fabrics on Answer panels, skins, and Hutch kit
tackboard is warp vertical. This is most com-
monly used on COMs designed for warp vertical
application.
For more information about the warp
direction of fabrics, see the Surface
Materials Reference Manual.
