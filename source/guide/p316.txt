Product Details
Two interior configura -
tions of Tall Storage are
available. Individual box has
a ledge on one side and
space for binder storage on
the opposite side. Shared
box is divided down the
center with half ledge and
half binder storage on each
side.
66"W and 78"W
individual boxes have a
center divider on the binder
storage side for additional
support.
Column height at
shortest level is 297⁄8"H.
Column will then adjust
upward 3" for leveling.
Height from floor to top of
box is approximately 45".
