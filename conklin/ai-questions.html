AI Questions

Use AI to create ideas and solve problems.

  1. 1 Describe the problem and what a good result looks like.
  2. 2 Ask for several ideas, not just one answer.
  3. 3 Push back: ask what could go wrong and what it missed.
  4. 4 You decide. Test the best idea before you act on it.
Choosing a safe AI tool
Is it approved for work?Use tools Conklin approves. Unsure? Ask Wes first.
What happens to my data?Check if it saves or learns from what you type. Turn that off if you can.
Who makes it?Pick a known company with a clear privacy policy.
Try it on a real problem

“Give me 10 ways to cut wait times on customer orders, then rank them by cost and effort.”

Then ask: “What are the risks? What did you miss?”
1

Watch out for

  • Confident answers that are wrong or made up.
  • One-sided or biased answers. Ask for other views.
  • Confidential or customer data: never paste it in.
  • Password requests, or links you didn't expect.
